📖 Planet Inc. — Field Manual

🌍 Planets & Tapping. Each planet makes its own resource. Tap the planet in the scene to mine it by hand, or buy buildings under Overview for passive income. Buildings get a permanent ×2 milestone bonus at levels 10, 25 and 50. Reach a planet's resource threshold to unlock the next one — the glowing worlds around your current planet are the nearest ones in line; tap an unlocked one to travel there.

🔬 Per-Planet Research. Each planet has its own two local research upgrades, paid for in that planet's own resource — separate from the Tech Tree.

🛒 Shop tab. Boosts are temporary, resource-bought (Overdrive, Tap Frenzy, Research Spark, Expedition Shield, Fortune's Favor). Ship Yard and Starlight Shop both spend Starlight Points on permanent upgrades — Ship Yard improves your fleet, Starlight Shop improves how each run starts and how fast you prestige. Automation Core and Galactic Exchange are expensive one-time unlocks for late-game convenience: auto-buying and cross-planet resource conversion.

🚀 Fleet tab. Send ships on expeditions (Short/Standard/Deep/Extreme/Interstellar — longer and riskier pays more). They keep running even while you're away. More ship slots unlock at 3 and 8 Starlight Points.

💎 Collection tab. Relics are permanent passive bonuses found on expeditions. Relic Fragments (also from expeditions) can be combined into a guaranteed new relic, or spent to permanently level up one you own. Missions rotate automatically and pay out resources (and sometimes fragments) on completion.

🏆 Progress tab. Starlight Prestige: "Collapse" resets your current planets' resources and buildings for permanent Starlight Points (+10% production each, forever). Research, Megastructure progress, relics, fragments, Quantum Cores, ship upgrades and Starlight Shop purchases all survive a Collapse — only the raw economy resets. The Leaderboard ranks everyone who has opened this game by lifetime resources earned.

🧬 Tech Tree. A separate, global research tree with three branches — Industry (raw production), Exploration (better expeditions), Ancient Tech (relics, offline earnings, cosmic events). Each branch is a straight chain: unlock a node to afford the next. The final node in each branch costs Quantum Cores instead of planet resources.

⚛️ Quantum Cores. The late-game currency. Earned from Deep/Extreme/Interstellar expeditions, Megastructure milestones, and Core Overcharge. Spent on Tech Tree capstones and the final Megastructure stage.

🏗️ Megastructure. A single shared 7-stage Dyson Sphere build. Each stage needs two resources from planets you'll already have unlocked by then — contribute 10%/25%/Max per resource. Some stages are gated behind Tech Tree progress. Completing it permanently boosts all production and unlocks Core Overcharge, a repeatable action for more Quantum Cores and Starlight.

⚡ Cosmic Surges. Random temporary production multipliers on a random unlocked planet. Ancient Tech research makes them trigger more often and hit harder; a late Megastructure stage adds a rare, stronger "Dyson Flare" variant.

⚔️ Frontier tab. Three linked systems. Fleet Construction builds actual ships (Scout → Fighter → Cruiser → Dreadnought → Flagship), each adding attack to your total Fleet Power. Higher classes need a Blueprint, bought with Blueprint Fragments plus planet resources. Alien Archaeology sends two dig teams on timed excavations (like expeditions, but digging) that mostly pay out resources, with a chance at a permanent Artefact (boosts dig speed, artefact odds, fleet attack, or boss drop odds) or Blueprint Fragments — so Archaeology directly funds Fleet Construction. Cosmic Bosses are fought with your Fleet Power: tap "Attack" for a burst of damage, or just leave it engaged — your fleet chips away at the boss passively too. Bosses unlock in order; once defeated, a boss stays refightable (on a cooldown) for repeat Quantum Core, Fragment, Starlight and Artefact drops. Ships, blueprints, artefacts and boss progress all survive a Collapse.

✨ Rumor has it something ancient still sleeps at the heart of a finished Dyson Core — for those patient and curious enough to knock on it the right number of times, with enough Quantum Cores in hand to answer back.
